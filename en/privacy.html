Privacy Policy

Henro Goshuin: Shikoku 88 (四国八十八ヶ所 御朱印めぐり帳) / Last updated: 2026-11-04

This app, “Henro Goshuin: Shikoku 88” (Japanese title: 四国八十八ヶ所 御朱印めぐり帳; the “App”), records the temple stamps (nōkyō), travel photos and journal entries you receive on the Shikoku 88 Temple Pilgrimage. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used. The Japanese version of this policy is the original; if the versions differ, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Provider

  • App name: Henro Goshuin: Shikoku 88 (四国八十八ヶ所 御朱印めぐり帳)
  • Provider: Dr. Hac
  • Contact: drhac.jp@gmail.com

2. Information handled and where it is stored

The App does not ask you to create an account (name, email address, etc.) and has no server of its own. It handles the following information.

The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number or contacts. It shows no ads and performs no usage analytics (no analytics SDK). The App never sends your nōkyō, photos, journal or location to the provider’s or any third party’s server. However, anything you send via “Report a correction to temple information” is stored in the provider’s Google Spreadsheet as described above and read by the provider to make corrections. Please do not include personal information in your message.

3. iCloud sync

If you have purchased the full version or are in a trial, your records are saved in your own iCloud (an Apple service) and synced between devices that use the same Apple ID. Data storage follows Apple’s privacy policy. If you do not want to use iCloud, you can turn off iCloud for the App in your device settings.

4. Links to external sites

From a temple page you can open each temple’s official website or Wikipedia, and launch Google Maps / Apple Maps for directions. Information on those sites and apps is handled under the policies of their respective operators. The App does not pass any of your information to linked destinations.

To let you know about new versions, the App checks the published version of the App on Apple’s App Store about once a day. Only the App’s App Store ID is sent in this check; none of your information is included.

5. Bundled content

Temple photos are Creative Commons images from Wikimedia Commons, bundled with their sources credited. Temple commentary was written by AI using the Shikoku 88 Pilgrimage Association (四国八十八ヶ所霊場会), temples’ official websites and encyclopedias as sources, and checked by a person. English and Spanish commentary was translated from the Japanese commentary by AI and checked by a person. If you find an error, please let us know via “Report a correction to temple information” in the App.

6. Purposes of use

  • Recording and displaying nōkyō, photos and journal entries; exporting the books and pilgrimage records
  • Suggesting the nearest temple and sorting by distance
  • Managing the status of in-app purchases

7. Provision to third parties

We do not provide or sell the information to third parties, except when disclosure is required by law. Payments are processed by Apple (App Store).

8. Your rights and deletion

  • You can delete nōkyō, photos, journal entries and visits within the App at any time.
  • You can export your records at any time via “Settings > Export records to this device (ZIP)”.
  • Deleting the App removes the records on the device. Records in iCloud can be deleted from your iCloud settings.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for general pilgrims and is not directed primarily at children. It does not collect personally identifiable information.

10. Disclaimer

The App does not grant nōkyō stamps; it is a record book for the nōkyō you receive at the temples. Visiting hours, nōkyō hours and fees are based on official information but may change. When visiting, please follow each temple’s guidance.

11. Changes

We will revise this policy as necessary. Important changes will be announced in the App or on this page.
