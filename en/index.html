Henro Goshuin: Shikoku 88 — Support

Provider: Dr. Hac

Henro Goshuin: Shikoku 88 (四国八十八ヶ所 御朱印めぐり帳) is an iPhone / iPad app for recording the temple stamps (nōkyō), travel photos and journal entries you receive on the Shikoku 88 Temple Pilgrimage — 88 temples across the four prefectures of Tokushima, Kōchi, Ehime and Kagawa. It is available in Japanese, English and Spanish. The App does not grant nōkyō stamps; it is a record book for the nōkyō you receive at the temples.

FAQ

Q. Where are my records stored?
Only on your device and in your own iCloud (if you have purchased the full version or are in a trial). Nothing is sent to the provider’s server.

Q. Can I keep my records when I change devices?
If iCloud sync is on with the same Apple ID, they carry over automatically. You can also save a copy via “Settings > Export records to this device (ZIP)”.

Q. Can I use my purchase of all 88 temples on another device?
Yes, use “Settings > Restore purchase” (the same Apple ID is required). Family Sharing is supported.

Q. Where do the temple photos come from?
They are Creative Commons images from Wikimedia Commons. Attributions are listed under “Settings > Photo credits”.

Q. Visiting or nōkyō hours differ from what I found.
They are based on official information but may change. Please let us know at the contact above, and follow each temple’s guidance when visiting.
